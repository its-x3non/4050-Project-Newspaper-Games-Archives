About

Nonogram is a puzzle game that involves filling in a grid based on numerical clues.

History

The origins of Nonogram can be traced back to Japan, where it was first published in the 1980s.

How to Play

To play Nonogram, you need to fill in the grid so that the numbers indicate how many consecutive squares in each row or column are filled.

Tips & Tricks

  • Start with the rows or columns that have the largest numbers, as they will give you more information to work with.
  • Use logic and deduction to figure out which squares must be filled in based on the clues provided.
  • Keep track of your progress and make notes if necessary to avoid confusion.
