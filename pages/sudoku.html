About

Sudoku is a logic-based number placement puzzle. The objective is to fill a 9x9 grid with digits so that each column, each row, and each of the nine 3x3 subgrids contain all of the digits from 1 to 9.

History

The origins of Sudoku can be traced back to the late 19th century, but it gained worldwide popularity in the early 2000s.

How to Play

To play Sudoku, you need to fill in the empty cells of the grid with numbers from 1 to 9, ensuring that no number is repeated in any row, column, or 3x3 subgrid.

Tips & Tricks

  • Start with the rows, columns, or subgrids that have the most numbers filled in, as they will give you more information to work with.
  • Use logic and deduction to figure out which numbers must go in each empty cell based on the existing numbers.
  • Keep track of your progress and make notes if necessary to avoid confusion.
